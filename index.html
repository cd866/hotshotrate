How HotshotRate works

  • All-miles RPM = Total rate ÷ (deadhead + loaded). Honest number — empty miles count.
  • Fuel = (total miles ÷ MPG) × $/gal. Tolls subtract from contribution.
  • Fees = lease-on % + factoring % of gross rate (typical lease-on ~5%).
  • True contribution = rate − fuel − tolls − fees — money left before fixed costs.
  • Net after fixed allocates truck note, insurance, trailer, ELD, and maintenance reserve across estimated monthly miles.
  • TAKE IT / MARGINAL / PASS vs your target on the basis you choose (after fees by default).

Privacy: Calculations and saved loads stay on this device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded. No signup. Free forever for owner-ops.
