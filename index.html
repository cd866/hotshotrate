Black Ram Logistics

Black Ram Logistics is the hotshot DBA of Sand Lily Pad LLC, leased onto Totni Enterprise’s MC authority.

Our rig: 2026 Ram 3500 dually and a 40 ft Big Tex gooseneck. Cargo up to 12,500 lb is OK; 12,501-15,300 lb is MAYBE (scale check, load centered just ahead of trailer axles); over 15,300 lb is a PASS. The Ram GVWR of 14,000 lb binds (CAT empty 10,940 lb on the truck). Tarps, straps. $/hr EST = net EST / hours. Hours = (loaded mi + empty miles) / 50 mph + 2 h load/unload per load. Net $/mi EST = net EST / (loaded mi + empty miles).

Load Boards shows the latest Truckstop and Direct Freight scan. Net EST is the posted rate times 0.87, minus $1.14 per loaded mile (fuel and DEF) and $0.30 per empty mile. Driver pay is not in the per-load estimate; Meshach is paid out of profits. A single load needs at least $500 net EST, or $300 for a same-day run under 250 total miles. A chain or a 2- or 3-load combo needs at least $1,000 total with no losing leg. Load Boards lists TAKE loads first, then MAYBE, then PASS. Tarp-required and F+T loads are a fit; the rig has tarps and straps. If a company email is listed, Email broker opens a draft in your mail app and does not send it. If there is no email, Call broker uses a company phone, or a looked-up company line marked Looked up. Direct Freight rows with no broker identity link to the board detail. Each load and combo shows a route map (grey dashed empty miles to pickup, solid purple loaded) and an Open in Google Maps directions link. Maps use OpenStreetMap and only load when they scroll into view. The public list does not include contact person names, personal cell numbers, card numbers, or other confidential details.
